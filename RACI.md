# Swarm Intelligence — Draft RACI Matrix

Project: Multi-Agent Orchestration App

## Legend

- R — Responsible: completes the work.
- A — Accountable: owns its completion.
- C — Consulted: provides advice or input.
- I — Informed: receives progress updates.
- A/R — Both accountable and responsible.

## Review and Updates

This is an initial allocation based on our current development roles.
All members will contribute outside their primary areas when needed.

We will review this matrix each project phase or sprint and rotate
accountability where appropriate to the work and sprint goals.

The faculty advisor will be consulted on technical design and evaluation.
The sponsor will be consulted on requirements and domain constraints
and informed of progress through project reviews.
